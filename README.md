A 100-word monologue for a fantasy villain who is secretly just very tired — written for the [make.fun bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/u-kevin-the-tired-dark-lord-a-100-word-villain-58b4f3cb).

## The monologue

> You'd think nine centuries ruling the Ashen Marches would teach a man to sleep. It hasn't. Ravens bring dawn reports: grain short in Vell, priests haggling over tithes, the general asking whether Caer Dusk can wait till spring. It can. It can't. I haven't decided, and not-deciding is the only quiet I get. My mother built this throne from the bones of kings who bored her. I sit in it because someone must sign the executions; the seal only answers my hand. So. Bring the map, the ink, the tea. The empire waited long. It can wait for one cup.

Exactly 100 words, 544 characters. The tiredness lives in the rhythm, the pauses and the small deflections — the Dark Lord never says he is weary. He is still a villain: nine centuries on a throne built from the bones of kings who bored his mother, executions to sign, and an invasion of Caer Dusk he keeps postponing one cup of tea at a time.

## How it works

`main.ts` writes the monologue to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output/entry.txt`) and refuses to run unless the text is exactly 100 words and within the gate of 1–600 characters.

```sh
node main.ts
```
